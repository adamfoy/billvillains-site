Common questions

How do I add my subscriptions quickly?

Tap Add, then "Find them on my bank statement". Copy transactions from your bank's app or website and paste them in, or choose a CSV file exported from your bank. Bill Villains finds the repeating charges and your paydays.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. It only reads text you paste or a file you choose, on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I move my villains to a new phone?

On the old phone, go to Settings, then "Save backup", and save the file to iCloud Drive or AirDrop it. On the new phone, go to Settings, then tap Restore under Backup, and choose that file.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Bill Villains. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

I'm not getting warnings.

Check that notifications are on for Bill Villains in your iPhone's Settings, and that "Villain warnings" is on in the app's Settings.
