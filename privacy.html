Bill Villains

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Bill Villains ("the app") is made by James Foy ("we", "us").

What we collect

Nothing you enter. The subscriptions, bills, prices, dates, paydays and name you add are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, never connects to your bank, and doesn't send your information to us.

Purchases

If you buy Premium, payment is handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your Premium is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, not your payment details or anything you entered in the app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Bank statements

If you paste transactions or choose a bank file to import, it is read on your device to find subscriptions and paydays. Only the items you choose to save are kept. The statement itself is not stored or sent anywhere.

Backups

If you choose "Save backup", the app creates a file with your data and hands it to the iPhone share sheet. Where it goes (iCloud Drive, Files, email, AirDrop) is up to you. We never receive it.

Face ID

If you turn on the lock, Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode is checked by iOS. The app only learns whether it succeeded. It never sees your face data or passcode.

Notifications and widgets

Bill warnings are scheduled on your device, not sent from a server. Widgets read a summary that the app saves on your device.

Sharing

Roast cards and other share images are made on your device and only go where you send them.

Links

Cancel guides open the service's own website in your browser. What happens there is covered by that website's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can turn off villain warnings in the app's Settings, delete all your data with "Delete all my data" in Settings (or by deleting the app), and cancel Premium in your App Store account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
